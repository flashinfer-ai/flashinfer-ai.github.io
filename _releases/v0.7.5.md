FlashInfer v0.7.5 brings DeepSeek-V4.1 sparse MLA decode, an MXFP8-output batched GEMM and Qwen3.8 MoE shapes to Rubin, and extends the batch-invariant mode to attention, block-scale FP8 GEMM, BF16 MoE and sampling log-probabilities on Blackwell and Rubin. Blackwell serving adds a Kimi K3 MegaMoE, more MLA decode options and tree-structured speculative verification for Gated DeltaNet; Hopper and Blackwell get a W8A16 FP8 GEMM, and RTX PRO 6000 gains native NVFP4 fused MoE operators.

### DeepSeek-V4.1 sparse MLA decode, MXFP8 projections and Qwen3.8 MoE shapes run on Rubin

The experimental Prims-TS decode kernel for DeepSeek-V4.1 sparse MLA runs on B200, B300 and Rubin SM107, reading the E4M3 sliding-window and packed FP4 compressed caches directly with CUDA Graph replay. `bmm_mxfp8(backend="cute-dsl")` on SM107 can write MXFP8 output with its block scales (`dtype=torch.float8_e4m3fn` plus `out_scale`), so DeepSeek-V4.1's grouped attention output projection feeds the next MXFP8 GEMM in one launch. The Prims-TS FP8 block-scale routed MoE accepts 32x32 weight-scale blocks on SM100, SM103 and SM107, the format of the tensor-parallel shard of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, and `BatchPrefillWithRaggedKVCacheWrapper(backend="cute-dsl-rubin")` adds opt-in E4M3 prefill with head dimensions 192 and 128 on SM107.

### Batch-invariant mode reaches attention, block-scale FP8 GEMM, BF16 MoE and sampling log-probabilities

With `batch_invariant=True`, `BatchDecodeWithPagedKVCacheWrapper`, `BatchPrefillWithPagedKVCacheWrapper` and `BatchPrefillWithRaggedKVCacheWrapper` return output bits that depend only on a token's KV position, head, window and input values, not on the batch, the page table or the SM count, with CUDA Graph replay (capture `run()` after `plan()`), on B200, B300 and Rubin, for BF16 causal attention with head dimension 64, 128 or 256. The same mode, passed as `batch_invariant=True`, covers `gemm_fp8_nt_groupwise`, `softmax`, `sampling.log_softmax` and `sampling.log_probs`, and `BatchInvariantBf16Config` adds a batch-invariant BF16 backend to `MoELayer`, selected explicitly through `BackendOptions`.

### Kimi K3 serving gains a MegaMoE and more MLA decode options on Blackwell

`flashinfer.moe_ep` adds a Kimi K3 and K3.1 Pro MegaMoE for the routed experts (E896, hidden 3584, intermediate 3072, top-16) through `Sm100_KimiK3_MegaMoeConfig`, at EP1, EP8 and EP16 with NVFP4 or MXFP4/MXFP8 precision and BF16 or MXFP8 combine; routing, latent projections, post-MoE RMSNorm and shared experts stay in the framework. Kimi K3 MLA decode (`trtllm_batch_decode_with_kv_cache_mla(backend="cake")`) adds a caller-owned LSE output and decode-context-parallel KV-shard masks on SM100 and SM103, and the `flashinfer.mla.cake_kimi_k3_mla` launchers add non-causal multi-token speculative verification.

### Gated DeltaNet verifies speculative-decode trees on Blackwell, and KDA decode takes fp32 state

`gated_delta_rule_mtp(verify_parents=...)` verifies tree-structured drafts (EAGLE-style, more than one child per node) on SM100 and SM103 through the ReplaySSM tcgen05 kernel, with an FP32 state pool and 4 to 8 tokens per request, and `gated_delta_rule_replayssm_commit(accept_paths=...)` commits an accepted root-to-node path that is not a prefix. `recurrent_kda`'s CuTe decode kernels follow the state dtype, so BF16 and FP32 state pools both run, and CuTe KDA prefill with an explicit `backend="cute-dsl"` reads token-strided Q, K and V views of packed projections directly on SM100 and SM120.

### FP8-weight GEMMs with 16-bit activations run on Hopper and Blackwell

`mm_bf16_fp8` and `mm_fp16_fp8` multiply BF16 or FP16 activations by an FP8 E4M3 `[N, K]` weight with one FP32 scale on H100 (`sm_90a`), B200 and B300, dequantizing the weight inside the kernel. The kernels compile on first use through CuTe DSL and take caller-supplied output and workspace buffers.

### NVFP4 fused MoE gains native operators on RTX PRO 6000

On RTX PRO 6000, the `sm12x_nvfp4` NVFP4 backend of `MoELayer` adds modular operators (Triton routing, fused CuTe DSL FC1 activation with quantization, CuTe DSL FC2 finalization) beside the existing monolithic kernel, plus SiTU and non-default SwiGLU limits. Devices with 188 SMs choose between them by shape; SiTU, non-default limits and explicit `enable_pdl=True` always use the modular operators.
