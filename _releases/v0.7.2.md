FlashInfer v0.7.2 brings `MoELayer` experts to Rubin through the cuDNN Frost backend, widens Rubin coverage across MLA, GEMM, linear attention and expert-parallel MoE, and adds fused-epilogue Prims-TS GEMMs on B200, B300 and Rubin. On Blackwell datacentre parts, DeepSeek-V4 NVFP4 sparse MLA reaches B200 and B300 and speculative-decode verification picks cuDNN automatically; expert-parallel MoE adds Hopper BF16 and SM120 W4A8 backends.

### `MoELayer` experts run on Rubin

`MoELayer` on Rubin SM107 can run its experts on cuDNN Frost, a CuTe DSL grouped-GEMM backend for BF16, MXFP8, NVFP4 and MXFP8-activation/MXFP4-weight experts; the BF16 kernels also run on Blackwell SM120. Autotuning admits Frost alongside the existing backends, and `CudnnFrostBf16Config`, `CudnnFrostMxfp8Config`, `CudnnFrostNvfp4Config` and `CudnnFrostMxfp8Mxfp4Config` in `flashinfer.fused_moe` select it explicitly. Frost needs PyTorch 2.14 (`CUDAGraph.retain_object`) and, on SM107, a PTXAS or CuTe DSL assembler from CUDA 13.4 or newer.

### DeepSeek-V4 and Kimi K3 serving kernels run on Rubin

`BatchMLAPagedAttentionWrapper(backend="auto")` plans on B300 and Rubin, and DeepSeek-V4 sparse MLA runs on SM107. Rubin also runs the CuTe DSL GEMM family, `mm_mxfp8(backend="cute-dsl")` on an SM107-specific kernel, the CuTe packed Kimi Delta Attention decode fast path, the CuTe DSL Mamba2 `SSDCombined` prefill, hardware stochastic rounding in the Mamba selective state update, and `silu_and_mul_mxfp8_quantize`. SM107 MegaMoE through `MoEEpLayer` adds MXFP4 weights with MXFP8 activations, prequantized NVFP4 and MXFP8 checkpoints, and Kimi K3's SiTU activation, functionally validated on Rubin.

### Prims-TS GEMMs fuse SwiGLU and QKV-norm-RoPE epilogues on B200, B300 and Rubin

`flashinfer.prims_ts` adds dense FP8 and NVFP4 GEMMs with fused SwiGLU (`fp8_linear_swiglu`, `fp4_linear_swiglu`) and fused QKV + QK-norm + RoPE (`fp8_qkv_qknorm_rope`, `fp4_qkv_qknorm_rope`) epilogues on B200, B300 and Rubin SM107. The NVFP4 SwiGLU and QKV variants accept an opt-in `tmem_overlap=True` tactic, which the autotuner also considers. The module needs the CuTe DSL experimental primitives shipped with `nvidia-cutlass-dsl`.

### DeepSeek-V4 NVFP4 sparse MLA reaches B200 and B300 and adds prefill on RTX PRO 6000

DeepSeek-V4 serving with an NVFP4 KV cache runs sparse-MLA decode on B200 and B300 through `trtllm_batch_decode_sparse_mla_dsv4(kv_cache_format="nvfp4", backend="cake")`. On RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell and RTX 5090 the same entry point gains a `backend="cake"` decode and prefill path for the NVFP4 cache and a decode path for the DeepSeek-V4.1 mixed FP8/FP4 cache (SM120 and GB10/SM121), plus fused RoPE, NVFP4 quantize and paged-insert cache writers. These backends are opt-in.

### Speculative-decode verification on B200 and B300 picks cuDNN automatically

`BatchDecodeWithPagedKVCacheWrapper(backend="auto")` on B200 and B300 routes multi-token decode rows (two to four query tokens per request, as in speculative or multi-token-prediction verification) of fp16/bf16 head-dim-128 grouped-query-attention models to cuDNN, which lowers verification decode latency. The route needs `nvidia-cudnn-frontend[cutedsl]>=1.31.0`. Single-token decode and runs with explicit determinism controls stay on FA2; `FLASHINFER_DECODE_AUTO_CUDNN=0` pins FA2.

### Expert-parallel MoE adds a BF16 Hopper backend and W4A8 on SM120

H100 serves an expert-parallel MoE round entirely in BF16 through `MoEEpLayer` with `Sm90_Bf16_Bf16_Bf16_PushCake_MegaMoeConfig`: BF16 dispatch, fused SwiGLU, a deterministic combine over a pre-reduced BF16 wire, CUDA Graph capture and up to 32 ranks on one node. SM120 workstations get a W4A8 MegaMoE backend (`Sm120_Mxfp4_Mxfp8_Bf16_Cutedsl_MegaMoeConfig`) with CUDA Graph replay and uneven token counts across ranks. `SplitConfig(comm=..., kernel=...)` picks an NVLink one-sided, CAKE or NVLink two-sided communication backend, or the NCCL-EP or NIXL-EP transports, independently of the expert kernel.

> **⚠️ Upgrade notices**
>
> - Install `nvidia-cudnn-frontend>=1.31.0`. The cuDNN linear-attention backend and the automatic cuDNN decode route need the `cutedsl` extra: `pip install -U 'nvidia-cudnn-frontend[cutedsl]>=1.31.0'`.
> - After upgrading `flashinfer-python`, run `flashinfer download-kernels` to replace mismatched `flashinfer-cubin` and `flashinfer-jit-cache` wheels; `--sm sm90a` (repeatable) limits it to named architectures.
> - Rename callers of `cutlass_fused_moe_prepare_workspace` to `cake_fused_moe_prepare_workspace`, replace `prepare_bgmv_moe(..., backend="blackwell")` with `backend="cake"`, and import the cuDNN Frost MoE configs from `flashinfer.fused_moe` (the `flashinfer.experimental` path is a one-release shim).
> - Sampling: batch-length `seed` and `offset` tensors apply one value per output row; callers passing distinct per-row values sample differently from before.

> **⚠️ Upgrade notices (Experimental)**
>
> - `flashinfer.source_mega_moe` (`MegaMoEPlan`, `prepare_mega_moe`) is removed; use `flashinfer.mega_moe_v3`.
> - `prepare_msa_nvfp4_sparse_decode` drops `workspace_buffer`; `msa_nvfp4_decode_workspace_size`, `workspace_layout` and `bind_decode_payload` are removed. DeepGEMM-family `prepare_*` entry points drop their descriptor arguments.
